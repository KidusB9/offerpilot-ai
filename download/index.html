Quick start

A native app. Three steps to running.

No Python, no keys, no config. Everything intelligent runs on the OfferPilot server, so the app is a small download that you unzip and run.

Unzip the download

Extract the OfferPilot folder anywhere (Desktop is fine). Keep the files together.

Run OfferPilot.exe

Double-click OfferPilot.exe. If Windows shows a one-time "protected your PC" notice, click More info → Run anyway (normal for new apps).

Sign in and go

Sign in with Google, pick a plan, and the copilot is ready. In the title bar, switch 🎤 mic ↔ 🔊 interviewer audio and cycle answer modes with Ctrl+Alt+Q.

High-accuracy on-device Whisper transcription is built in, nothing to download. Your audio and screen stay on your machine; only the transcribed text is sent to get an answer.
