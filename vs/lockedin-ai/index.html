Metering is where these tools quietly differ

Most copilots bill you for a session or a wall-clock window. Start the tool, spend forty minutes in an interview where the interviewer talks for twenty, and you are charged for forty. OfferPilot meters listening time: a heartbeat only counts a minute when the copilot is actually receiving interviewer audio. Pause it, take a call, think in silence, and the clock is not running. On a 3 hour a day plan that difference is the difference between a plan you can leave open and one you ration.

The AI is included, on every plan

OfferPilot never asks for an API key of your own. Grok, DeepSeek and Gemini all answer each question in parallel, once, and switching between the three answers is a display toggle that re-asks nothing. Gemini is the model we recommend for LeetCode-style problems; it produces cleaner optimal solutions and more reliable complexity analysis than the other two.

Where LockedIn AI is the better buy

LockedIn AI is a more established brand with a longer public track record, runs on macOS as well as Windows, and offers a lifetime option if you genuinely want to pay once and never think about it again. OfferPilot is Windows-only and newer. If you need macOS, this comparison ends here.
