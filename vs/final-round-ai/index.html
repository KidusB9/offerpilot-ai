Sessions vs a daily allowance

Final Round AI's monthly plan is reported at $149 for five copilot sessions. If you have one onsite loop this month, five sessions may be exactly right. If you are in an active search with screens, technicals and follow-ups every week, a session count becomes something you ration. OfferPilot sells a daily allowance instead: 3 hours a day on Pro at $39, 6 hours a day on Ultimate at $69, and the clock only runs while the copilot is actually listening to the interviewer.

What you give up

Final Round AI has the broader preparation product: mock interviews, structured feedback, recordings, reports and a set of AI career coaches across formats. If what you want is a coach that grades you over weeks, that is a real product and OfferPilot does not replace it. OfferPilot's preparation is narrower and specific: a company-and-role question bank generated from your own résumé, which then grounds the live answers.

What you gain

  • Invisible on every plan. Operating-system capture exclusion, including on the free tier, so you can verify it before paying.
  • On-device transcription. Interview audio is not shipped to a server to be turned into text.
  • Coding assessments as a first-class flow. Capture a problem across several scrolls, server-side OCR stitches it, and you get an optimal solution with intuition, a dry run and Big-O.
  • A quarter of the monthly cost at the entry paid tier.
