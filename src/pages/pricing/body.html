Pricing questions

Before you upgrade.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Pro and Ultimate are month-to-month with no contract. Cancel from your account in a couple of clicks and you keep full access until the end of the billing period you already paid for. Nothing renews after that.

Do you offer refunds?

Every paid plan, Pro, Ultimate, and Lifetime, is covered by a 7-day refund window. If OfferPilot is not right for you, email us within seven days of purchase and we will refund you in full, no interview-length interrogation required.

What counts as a minute?

A minute is counted only while the live copilot is actively listening and generating answers in a session. Generating a company Q&A set, browsing your prep, and capturing coding problems with Code Copilot do not touch your live-minute budget, so the Free tier's 20 minutes a day go entirely to real practice or a real round.

Is my data private?

Transcription runs fully on-device and your audio is never uploaded. On Ultimate and Lifetime you can route answers through a local, offline model for a completely private path where nothing leaves your machine. Cloud models are optional and only used for the models you explicitly choose.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, upgrade or downgrade whenever you like. Upgrades take effect immediately and we prorate the difference. Downgrades apply at your next billing date. Lifetime buyers are simply on Ultimate permanently.

Which platforms are supported?

The desktop copilot runs on Windows and macOS. Screen-share invisibility and coding capture are strongest on Windows 10 (2004+) and 11. Every plan works on both.
