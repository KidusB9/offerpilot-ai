Screen-share safe

Visible to you. Invisible to the call.

The overlay opts out of screen capture at the OS level using WDA_EXCLUDEFROMCAPTURE on Windows. It renders on your monitor but is composited out of the frame every capture tool receives.

  • ✦ Excluded from Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, Discord, OBS, Snipping Tool & PrintScreen
  • ✦ Stays out of the taskbar and Alt-Tab so its presence never leaks
  • ✦ A live shield badge tells you the truth: green when hidden, a banner if not
  • ✦ Ghost-mode hotkey dims it instantly; a global hotkey hides it entirely
display affinity · self-healing🛡 excluded
// Windows: opt the window out of capture
user32.SetWindowDisplayAffinity(
    hwnd, WDA_EXCLUDEFROMCAPTURE);

// re-asserted on a timer so it self-heals
// if Qt ever recreates the native window
status = exclude_from_capture(overlay);

Questions

The things everyone asks first.

Is it really undetectable during screen sharing?

On Windows the overlay uses SetWindowDisplayAffinity(WDA_EXCLUDEFROMCAPTURE), so it renders on your monitor but is composited out of the frame Zoom, Teams, Meet, Discord, OBS, and PrintScreen capture. It also stays out of the taskbar and Alt-Tab. A live shield badge tells you the current protection state so you're never falsely reassured.

What does OfferPilot cost?

Free gives you 20 minutes a day with no card. Pro is $19 a month, Ultimate is $29 a month with unlimited use, and Lifetime access is a one-time $299. You can start on the free tier and upgrade only if you need more time.

What does the company-specific Q&A generator do?

You pick a company and role; OfferPilot builds a tailored database of behavioral, fundamentals, and coding questions with model answers in your voice. During the live interview the copilot retrieves from that database so every answer fits the role and the company's domain.

Does anything leave my computer?

Transcription runs fully on-device. You can also run answers against a local model for a completely offline path. Cloud models are optional and only used for the models you choose.

Which platforms are supported?

The desktop copilot runs on Windows and macOS. The coding capture and screen-share invisibility are strongest on Windows 10 (2004+) and 11.
