Questions

How the live copilot works.

How does it answer in real time?

An on-device Vosk recognizer transcribes the interviewer as they speak. The moment they stop, your résumé and the company Q&A database are retrieved and fed to the model, and a speakable answer streams into the overlay in about a second. No clip uploads and no round-trip latency.

Can the interviewer see the overlay on screen share?

No. On Windows the overlay uses an OS-level screen-capture exclusion, so it renders on your monitor but is composited out of the frame Zoom, Teams, Meet, Discord, OBS, and PrintScreen capture. It also stays out of the taskbar and Alt-Tab, and a live shield badge shows the current protection state.

What are the answers grounded in?

Every live answer is retrieved from your résumé plus a company-specific Q&A database of behavioral, fundamentals, and coding questions with model answers in your voice. You build that set in seconds with the Company Q&A Generator above.

Which AI models does it use?

It queries Grok, DeepSeek, and Gemini, and can also run against a local model for a fully offline path. You choose which models to use, and only the ones you pick are called.

What do the hotkeys do?

M pauses the copilot instantly so it stops listening and answering; P toggles ghost mode to dim the overlay. Both are global hotkeys, so you never touch the window during the call.
